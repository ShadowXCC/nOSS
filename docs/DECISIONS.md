| D43 | 2026-10-10 | Conformance runner (PLT-06) | **Native maneuvers, GDScript orchestration.** GDScript loads platforms and fits synthetic `fit.json`s with the game's own `PlatformLoader` and `Fitting`; `NossConformance.run_batch` runs the maneuvers on a flat-ground harness on all threads. The scripted driver follows paths with the CoM and commands curvature through the reference wheelbase. Metrics are interpolated between ticks; pass = ±2% of the reference config (yaw rise time: or 4 ms). An engine check compares the harness with Jolt (±5%). | No C++ copies of the loader or fitting to drift. About 2 s per platform, so it runs with every test. Jolt and the harness agree within 0.4%. |
| D44 | 2026-10-10 | Yaw normalization (supersedes the steering rule in PLATFORMS §4.2) | **Grip forces act at the reference arms.** Tire slip velocities and the yaw moment of the tires' ground-plane forces use the hub positions scaled to `L_ref` and `T_ref`. Yaw inertia and steering are the reference's (`δ` no longer scales with `L`). | Scaling steering by `L/L_ref` can't keep yaw dynamics equal: the bicycle model's stability term goes with `1/L`. Measured on GT: step-steer rise time ±9%, slalom −3.3%. After: < 0.3%. Cost: slight wheel scrub in tight turns on cars far from the reference wheelbase. |
| D45 | 2026-10-10 | Vertical normalization (R2) | Extra pitch moment so longitudinal load transfer keeps the reference's `h/L`. Effective track weighted by roll share; per-axle roll stiffness `share · (T_ax/T) · K_φ` with a signed bar (no clamp at 0); roll inertia `∝ T · T_ref`; a roll damping bar; CoG measured from where the wheels touch down. | Before: 0–100 km/h −8.8%, 100–0 m +4.8% at the grid corners (the known residual R2). After: < 0.35%. Wide geometry ranges stay, so few models get scaled. |
| D46 | 2026-10-10 | TCS at a standing start | TCS ignores wheelspin while the clutch is slipping. | Found by the runner: on some fits TCS cut the throttle at launch, the engine couldn't rev past idle, the auto clutch never engaged and the car sat still. |
| D47 | 2026-10-10 | AWD and per-axle diffs | `differential.center` (`open`/`viscous`/`locked`, `front_share`) under `differential`, with `front`/`rear` overrides for the axles. Drive torque reaches each driven wheel by its share; each axle's LSD locks on the torque that axle gets. | Keeps all diff settings in one block, so tuning paths like `differential.center.front_share` work. Fixed on the way: each axle's LSD used to see the whole input torque. |
| D48 | 2026-10-10 | Kart chassis and stiff springs | `suspension.type: "rigid"` (stiff contact spring, no visible travel) instead of a separate physics path. Springs, dampers, bump stops and over-travel rates are capped for the 120 Hz tick. Wheel inertia from the platform or `0.0085 · m · r²`. | Same code path, so normalization and conformance apply to karts too. A fixed 1.2 kg·m² wheel inertia was like 245 kg of extra mass on a 170 kg kart. |
| D49 | 2026-10-10 | Physics wheels | The platform's layout decides the physics wheels; `Fitting` builds them from the model's first and last axle, and maps every model wheel to the physics wheel(s) it shows. `strict` puts them at the reference wheelbase, track and radius (visual radius only for spin). `stock` wheels are generated, `static` ones stay still. | A 3-wheel or 3-axle model used to become 3-wheel or 3-axle physics (against §4.4), and hotswapping between them changed the sim's wheel count. Also fixed: CarVisual moved and lifted wheels in the source model's axes, which breaks for any rotated model. |
| D50 | 2026-10-10 | LOD generation | Native **meshoptimizer 1.3** (`NossMesh.simplify_surface`), four levels at 50/25/10/4% (the last sloppy), error relative to the car's length, tiny parts dropped from LOD2 on, every node kept. Textureless LODs keep packages small and add no texture memory. A 219k-triangle car gets its LODs in about 0.5 s. |
| D51 | 2026-10-10 | Model import (R3) | Keep a clean GLB byte for byte; re-export everything else through Godot (`FBXDocument` for FBX, `GLTFDocument` for `.gltf` and GLBs that fail the checks). | D52 | 2026-10-10 | Fitting without prep | Wheels by shape (round, thin parts with a shared axle), names only as evidence; orientation from the wheels; scale from the wheelbase; merged wheels split out of the body; detected wheels get a pivot made at load time (`pivot: "insert"`) instead of editing the model. | Most free models don't follow any naming. | D53 | 2026-10-10 | Built-in engine sounds (AV-05) | **Synthesized**, not recorded: a native synth (`NossAudio`) renders seamless loops from each profile's firing order, exhaust banks and resonances; the GDScript player cross-fades rpm points and on/off-load per emitter and adds the tweak layers. | Owner's choice. | D57 | 2026-10-10 | Focus in player menus | Godot keeps **one focused control per OS window**, even across SubViewports. | D63 | 2026-10-11 | Racers and sessions (M4) | **`Racer`** holds what every car in a session has (car loading and hotswap, lap timer, reset spot, race state); **`SessionPlayer`** adds a local player's view, HUD, controls and menus; **`AiRacer`** is a computer driver with no view. FreeDrive became **`TrackSession`**. | D64 | 2026-10-11 | AI racing line (AI-01) | A **minimum-curvature line**: lateral offsets per centreline sample relaxed (squared second differences, Gauss-Seidel) coarse to fine (32 m, 8 m, 2 m) inside the road less 1.3 m, with 0.5 m of curb; about 0.1 s per track. A **speed profile per platform** from grip (with downforce), a power-limited forward pass and a braking pass capped by **brake balance** (the rear runs out of grip first under load transfer). | The raw centreline's curvature spikes at its control points. Without the brake-balance cap the AI braked harder than the GT's 62% front bias allows and spun under braking. |
| D65 | 2026-10-11 | AI traffic rules (AI-01, "no pile-ups") | Pure pursuit on the line plus an offset (with corner-cut compensation, look²κ/8), speed from the profile with the profile's deceleration as brake feed-forward, slide control (ease brakes, counter-steer). Around other cars: a time gap with the speed difference's braking distance; passes only with room and, in braking zones, only if already alongside, keeping the follow limit until clear of the other car's lane; **cars alongside each keep to their side of the midpoint between them**; emergency braking when the gap is less than the stopping distance at the closing speed; one defensive move per straight; yield to blue flags; hold the grid column for 200 m, extra caution on lap 1. Skill 0–100 sets grip use (78–95%), following gap and line precision, never grip or power. The AI uses the assists players may use on the platform, stability control included. | Measured with `tools/race_check.sh`: 12 cars × 5 laps over 12 seeds, four classes and three misbehaving stand-ins (stalls on the grid, brakes early into Turn 1, spins): no pile-ups. The midpoint rule fixed two cars both merging toward the line at once. |
| D66 | 2026-10-11 | Resets in traffic | **Safe rejoin**: a reset car waits hidden, without collisions, until nothing is within 4 s upstream (10 s at most), then appears at the road's edge away from the line and stays ghosted (no contact, see-through) for 3 s. Ghosting changes the collision **layer**. Held cars wait on the **handbrake**. | Resetting backwards along the track put cars in front of oncoming traffic. With the automatic gearbox, holding the brake at a standstill selects reverse, so a grid held on the brake drove backwards into itself. |
| D67 | 2026-10-11 | Race rules (MODE-02) | Grid: pole 12 m behind the line, cars in a column 13 m apart (rolling start 220 m further back at 80 km/h, green 60 m before the line). Laps count from the first crossing; the race clock starts at the green. After the leader finishes, others finish their lap (60 s cap); practice and qualifying let the lap in progress finish when time is up. **Track limits**: out when all four wheels are past the edge plus the curb; practice and qualifying laps are invalidated; in races a cut **gained time** if the stretch was driven faster than 98% of the line profile's time for it (warning first, then +2 s lenient or +5 s strict; strict counts every cut). Yellow: a stopped or slow car within 250 m ahead; blue: a car most of a lap up within 100 m behind. | Simple to explain, and a car running wide never gains on the ideal line, so only real cuts are punished. |
| D68 | 2026-10-11 | Stability control (INP-07) | Native: compares the measured yaw rate with a neutral-steer target capped by grip; turning too far or sliding past 4° brakes the outside front and trims the throttle; turning much less than an understeering car would (characteristic speed 25 m/s) brakes the inside rear. Off in the conformance driver. | Cut peak sideslip in a trail-braking test from 20° to 11°, and stays out of a steady corner. |
| D69 | 2026-10-11 | Ghosts and leaderboards | Ghosts record position, rotation, velocity, steer and speed at 30 Hz (13 floats a sample), replayed with **Hermite** interpolation; saved as JSON with the samples in base64 (about 150 KB a lap), checked on load (schema, sizes, finite numbers, time order). The live delta compares against the ghost when it's faster than the session's best. Leaderboards are per track file, a list per platform, and take only official platforms at full game speed. | Linear interpolation at 30 Hz cuts corners; Hermite stays within millimetres. The same AI on the next lap stays within 0.2 m of its ghost. |
| D70 | 2026-10-11 | Setups (PLT-04) | Setups store only changes, clamped and snapped to the platform's ranges and steps on load; gears stay in order (0.05 apart); applied by re-configuring the sim (`Vehicle.apply_setup`), which keeps the car moving; the platform hash stays the official one and results mark "Setup".
